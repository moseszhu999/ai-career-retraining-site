Attestation trust levels

The contract distinguishes who attested the credential.

This avoids pretending every on-chain hash has the same trust value.

Level 1

Self-attested

Holder generated evidence and registered proof themselves.

Verifier meaning

Good for portfolio or learning record, not formal certification.

Level 2

Issuer-attested

Authorized issuer wallet reviewed and registered the proof.

Verifier meaning

Suitable for training, employment screening, or paid certificate.

Level 3

Evaluator-signed

Issuer plus evaluator signature set, represented by evaluatorSetHash.

Verifier meaning

Strongest project-based credential for B2B and hiring scenarios.
